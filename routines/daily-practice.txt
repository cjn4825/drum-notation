PART 1: PRACTICE PAD (5 MINS PER SECTION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STAGE 1: WARMUP & FOUNDATIONS @ 60 BPM
  8-on-a-Hand:       R R R R R R R R | L L L L L L L L (K 1/2/3/4)
  Single Stroke:     R L R L R L R L | R L R L (Single Four)
  Double Stroke:     R R L L R R L L | R R L L R R L L R (9-Stroke)
  Single Paradiddle: R l r r | L r l l

STAGE 2: DIDDLES, FLAMS & DRAGS @ 70 BPM
  Double Paradiddle: R L R L R R | L R L R L L
  Paradiddle-Diddle: R L R R L L | R L R R L L
  5-Stroke Roll:     R R L L R | L L R R L
  6-Stroke Roll:     R L L R R L
  Flam & Flam Tap:   lR | rL | lR R | rL L
  Flam Accent:       lR L R | rL R L
  Drag & Drag Tap:   rrL | llR | rrL R | llR L
  14-Rudiment Flow:  Cycle all 14 back-to-back

STAGE 3: SPEED & ACCENTS @ 90+ BPM
  Stroke Integration: R L R L R L R L | R R L L R R L L
  Dynamic Accents:    R l r r | L r l l | R L L R R L
  Speed Bursts:       R R L L R | R R L L R R L L R | R L R R L L
  Burnout:            R L R L | R R L L (Slow -> Fast -> Slow)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2: DRUM KIT (5 MINS PER SECTION) @ 70-80 BPM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STAGE 1: KIT ORCHESTRATION
  Singles & Doubles:   R -> High Tom | L -> Snare
                       R R -> Floor Tom | L L -> Snare
  Paradiddle & 6-Str:  R -> Floor | L -> High Tom | r r / l l -> Snare
                       R L L R R L (Accents on outer R/L)
  Flams, Drags & Rolls: rrL R -> Ride Bell | R R L L R -> Crash+Kick

STAGE 2: GROOVE DYNAMICS
  Ghost Notes:         R l r r L r l l | rrL R llR L (Keep 8th Hi-Hat)

STAGE 3: GROOVE TO FILL TRANSITIONS
  4-Bar Loop:
    Bars 1-3 (Groove): Hi-Hat (R 8ths) | Snare (L on 2/4) | Kick (1/3)
    Bar 4 (Fill):      R L L R R L | R L R R L L | R R L L R
    Bar 5 (Reset):     R (Crash + Kick on Beat 1)
